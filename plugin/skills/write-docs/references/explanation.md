# Explanation: why it is this way

The reader wants to understand: the design, the trade-offs, the limits. They
are not in the middle of a task.

Belongs:
- The question the page answers, in the first lines.
- How the thing works at the level the reader needs, and why it is shaped this
  way, including the alternatives that were rejected and what they would cost.
- Honest limits: what it cannot do.
- Links to the decision records in `.okf/decisions/` behind the design.

Does not belong:
- Steps to follow; link the how-to.
- Exhaustive option lists; link the reference.
