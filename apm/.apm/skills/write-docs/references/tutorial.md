# Tutorial: a lesson that works in one sitting

The reader is new and wants to learn by doing. You are responsible for their
success: every step must work exactly as written, in order.

Belongs:
- One small, concrete result the reader builds, named at the start.
- What they need before they start, with how to get it.
- Numbered steps, each one action and what they should see after it.
- A short "what you learned" that names what to read next.

Does not belong:
- Choices and alternatives ("you could also..."): pick one path.
- Explanation of why beyond one sentence; link the explanation page.
- Every option of a command; link the reference page.
